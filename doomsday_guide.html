Eight things to know

Explain these eight and you will follow the film.

VariantAnother version of a character.
TimelineA sequence of events, a history.
BranchAn alternate path that splits from a timeline.
MultiverseMany realities existing alongside one another.
TVAThe Time Variance Authority, the organization that deals with timelines and multiversal threats.
IncursionUniverses colliding or interfering in a way that can lead to catastrophic destruction.
Different EarthsThe Avengers, the Fantastic Four and the legacy X-Men do not necessarily come from the same reality.
Doctor DoomVictor von Doom, played by Robert Downey Jr. Marvel describes him as Victor von Doom, not as Tony Stark returning.
